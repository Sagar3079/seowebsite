Chef & sourcing

Cooking at the speed of the harbor

The menu is written twice — once by me in the afternoon, and once by the ocean that morning.
— Dario Ventimiglia, chef & co-owner

Dario cooked nine years on the Amalfi coast and three at Providence in Los Angeles before opening Saffron & Vine with sommelier Maren Ellis in 2019. The kitchen burns olive and almond wood, buys whole animals, and keeps exactly one freezer — for the gelato.
