Care guide

Five habits, ten extra days

Printed on the back of every care card since we opened in 2019. The order matters — that's the routine, start to finish.

Trim on arrival

Two centimetres off every stem, cut at 45° under running water. Blunt scissors bruise; use a knife if you have one.

Strip the waterline

No leaf below the surface, ever. Submerged foliage is what turns vase water into soup by Thursday.

Cold, clean, daily

Fresh cold water every day beats flower food every time. Rinse the vase — film on glass feeds bacteria.

Mind the fruit bowl

Ripening fruit exhales ethylene, and ethylene ends flowers. Keep arrangements a table away from bananas.

Edit as you go

Pull spent blooms the moment they slump. A smaller, editing-down arrangement always outlives a crowded one.
