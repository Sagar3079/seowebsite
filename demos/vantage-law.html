Practice areas

Six disciplines. One posture: trial-ready from day one.

We staff every matter as if it will be tried, because opposing counsel can tell when you don't.

I

Complex Commercial

Contract, fiduciary-duty and business-tort disputes where nine figures ride on the outcome — for plaintiffs and defendants alike.

Corvale Systems v. Aldon Group — $38M ICC award for a jilted technology supplier, 2025.

II

Securities & Shareholder

10b-5 class actions, M&A litigation and derivative suits — prosecuted and defended before the country's most demanding benches.

In re Meridian Capital — $212M jury verdict for a certified investor class, S.D.N.Y. 2024.

III

White Collar & Investigations

Grand-jury matters, DOJ and SEC enforcement, and board-level internal investigations, handled with discretion and speed.

Fortune 250 CFO — full declination after a 14-month cross-border inquiry, 2024.

IV

Antitrust & Competition

Price-fixing, monopolization and merger challenges — plus the private damages actions that follow them.

Halloran v. TriState Freight — $94M settlement for a class of 1,900 shippers, 2023.

V

Intellectual Property

Patent, trade-secret and licensing trials in the venues where they are actually won — W.D. Tex., D. Del. and the ITC.

Averline v. Corax Semiconductor — complete defense verdict, W.D. Tex. 2024.

VI

Appellate & Critical Motions

Preserving wins and reversing losses. Our appellate group briefs the case the trial team actually tried.

14 circuit arguments since 2020 — including a 2nd Cir. reversal reinstating a $61M judgment.
