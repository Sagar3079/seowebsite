Bespoke

A piece that exists once

Commissions begin at $6,000 and take eight to fourteen weeks. Four meetings, one ring — or brooch, or tiara; we don't judge ambition.

I

The Conversation

Week 1 · Florence, New York, or by video

An hour with a designer and strong coffee. Bring photographs, heirlooms, a sentence, or nothing but the occasion. We take notes in pencil.

II

The Gouache

Weeks 2–4 · Two revisions included

Your piece is painted at actual size in gouache on grey paper, the way this house has presented designs since its first commission. You approve the painting before metal is touched.

III

The Wax

Weeks 5–8 · One fitting

A carved wax model, sized to your hand, fitted in person or by post. This is where millimetres are argued about, and should be.

IV

The Setting

Weeks 9–14 · Hallmarked & delivered

Stones are set under the loupe, the band is finished and struck with the Aurea hallmark and your edition mark, and the piece is delivered in its walnut vitrine box. We keep the gouache; you keep everything else.

Every commission includes a lifetime of cleaning, sizing, and re-tipping — bring it home once a year and we treat it like it never left.
