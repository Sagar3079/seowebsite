The Ritual of the Chair

A shave here follows the same order it has since barbering was surgery's quieter cousin. Every minute is part of the work.

00:00

The Reading

Grain mapped by hand, skin assessed, blade chosen. Nothing touches your face before this.

00:05

First Heat

Two towels, steamed over eucalyptus. Pores open, bristle softens, shoulders drop.

00:12

The Lather

Tallow soap worked in a warm bowl for a full three minutes, painted with the grain.

00:18

The Passes

With the grain, then across it. The razor is honest — it takes exactly what it is asked to.

00:38

Cold Close

Iced towel, alum block, unscented balm. You leave with the face you were promised.

House Rules

  • Be on time. Ten minutes late is a rebooking, not a shorter cut.
  • Phones on silent. The chair is the last quiet room in Brooklyn. Keep it that way.
  • Say what you want. Bring a photo, bring a sentence, bring a doubt — we work with all three.
  • Kids from twelve. A straight razor room is no place for a birthday party.
  • The barber's word is final on the fade line. You chose a professional; let him be one.
  • Tip in cash if you can. The card machine takes a cut. So do we, but ours is better earned.
