Nine years, five kitchens, one question

Our story

  1. October 2017

    The library fire alarm

    Week three at Bristol. A burnt panini empties the Arts & Social Sciences library at 11pm, and June lends a very cold stranger her scarf. Theo still has it.

  2. June 2019

    The allotment

    We sign for half an allotment in Easton with no idea what we're doing. The first year yields eleven radishes and one enormous marrow nobody asked for.

  3. March 2021

    Flat no. 3, Cotham

    We move in together during a rainstorm with one van, two sourdough starters, and June's piano, which takes four neighbours and a pulley to get upstairs.

  4. August 2024

    Theo asks, badly

    Halfway up Glastonbury Tor, Theo drops the ring in the long grass mid-sentence. Twenty minutes of searching later, June says yes anyway.

  5. September 2026

    We marry in a garden

    Back in Somerset, twenty minutes from the Tor, with everyone we love and considerably better weather, we hope.

The practical bits

Questions, answered

What should I wear?

Garden formal: suits without obligation of ties, dresses you can dance in, and shoes that forgive grass. The lawn is real lawn — consider block heels over stilettos.

Are children invited?

We love your small people, but apart from our nieces and nephews the day is adults-only. Consider it a night off — the coach gets you back to Bruton by midnight.

How do I get there, and can I park?

Ashcombe is ten minutes from Bruton station. Coaches leave the Market Place at 12:45pm sharp and return at 11:30pm. There's parking in the paddock if you'd rather drive — just don't leave your car overnight past 10am Sunday.

What if it rains?

It's Somerset in September, so: possibly. The ceremony moves into the glasshouse, umbrellas appear as if by magic, and the band plays either way.

Can I take photos during the ceremony?

We'd love the ceremony unplugged — our photographer, Ivy, will share everything. From the champagne onwards, snap away and send us the terrible ones.
