The desk

Serial 3417. Forty-seven years in service.

Our Neve 8048 was installed at Basing Street, London in 1978 and has never been modified beyond recapping. Every channel you hear on a Resonance record passed through these transformers.

  • 01Source → 1073 preamp, gain staged to tape
  • 0231102 EQ — broad strokes only, in the room
  • 03Bus → Studer A800 at 15 ips, +6 over 250 nWb
  • 04Monitor mix printed nightly. No recalls, no regrets.
