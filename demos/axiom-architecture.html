Process

Fixed sequence · 04 gates
Gate 01

Listen & survey

Weeks 0–4. Site walks at dawn and dusk, a measured survey, planning review, and the questions clients forget to ask themselves.

Deliverable · Site dossier

Gate 02

Massing

Weeks 4–10. Three options tested in the daylight model you see above — each with an honest cost band, not a hopeful one.

Development

Weeks 10–28. Materials, details and consultants coordinated in a single federated model. Nothing drawn twice, nothing guessed.

Studio

Est. 2014

Axiom Atelier was founded in 2014 by Mara Voss and Elias Trent, who met arguing about a stair. The studio occupies a former meatpacking loft at 1117 W Fulton Market and keeps a workshop on the ground floor — every project is modeled physically before it is modeled digitally.

We take on eight commissions a year, never more. Half our built work is public or non-profit, by policy rather than accident.
