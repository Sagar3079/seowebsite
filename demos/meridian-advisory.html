What we do

One plan, kept current for decades.

Everything below is included in one relationship and one transparent fee — there is no menu of add-ons, and no meter running when you call.

I.Retirement architecture

Withdrawal sequencing, Roth conversion windows, and Social Security timing modeled against your actual spending — revisited every year, not filed away.

II.Investment stewardship

Low-cost, globally diversified portfolios rebalanced by policy, not by headlines. Average fund expense across client accounts: 0.07%.

III.Tax coordination

Asset location, gain harvesting, and charitable bunching, coordinated directly with your CPA each October — before year-end, when it still matters.

IV.Estate & family continuity

Titling, beneficiary hygiene, and family meetings that make your intentions legible to the people who will carry them out.

V.Equity compensation

RSU and option exercise plans for executives at the port, the hospital systems, and the tech firms along the Ashley — built around 10b5-1 windows.

VI.Insurance review

We audit coverage we will never sell you. When something is missing, you buy it directly, at market rates, from whomever you choose.

Fee philosophy

If you can't explain the fee, it's too clever.

We publish our full schedule because we think you should be able to price us before you ever shake our hands. Fees are billed quarterly, in arrears, and appear as a line item — never netted quietly out of returns.
