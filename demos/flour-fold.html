The daily bake

A day at Flour & Fold, by the clock

Come at the right hour and your croissant is still warm. This schedule holds Wednesday through Sunday.

4:30 AM

Ovens on, levain fed

Yesterday's laminated doughs come out of retard. The sourdough gets its final shaping.

5:15 AM

First viennoiserie bake

Croissants, pains au chocolat, and kouign-amann — tray one of three.

7:00 AM

Doors open

The first bake hits the case still warm. Drip coffee is $3, and it is good.

8:45 AM

Second bake

The mid-morning tray, timed for the school-run crowd. Baguettes go in.

11:30 AM

Tarts & entremets finish

Citron tarts get their meringue; whole-cake pre-orders are boxed for pickup.

2:00 PM

Sold out, usually

Whatever remains goes to Blanchet House. Tomorrow's butter block gets its first fold.
