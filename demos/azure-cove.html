One island. One reef. Nowhere to be.

Azure Cove is a sixteen-villa island a forty-minute seaplane hop south of Malé. No lobby, no schedule — just sand paths, a house reef forty metres off your deck, and staff who learn your name before your bags arrive.

Getting there

Forty minutes over a hundred atolls

Land at Malé (MLE), walk 200 m to the seaplane terminal, and we take it from there — bags, check-in, cold towels. The flight south over Vaavu and Meemu is the best welcome we could design, so we didn't design anything else.

Departures daily at 09:15 and 13:45; returns 10:30 and 15:00. $420 return per guest, booked with your stay. Private charters on request.

Good to know

Island notes

Seasons & rates

May–November is our green-season sweet spot: villa rates from $780, glassy lagoons in the morning, dramatic skies at dusk. December–April peak rates run 35–50% higher; New Year requires a 7-night minimum.

Children & quiet

Families are welcome in the Sandbar Residence and Reef House year-round. The Lagoon Pool Villas and the Coral Spa deck stay adults-only, so honeymooners keep their hush.

Reef promise

Reef-safe sunscreen is in every villa (and the only kind allowed in the water). Our resident marine biologist, Dr. Leena Rasheed, runs a free house-reef walk every Tuesday at low tide.

Check your dates
