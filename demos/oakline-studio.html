Materials & joinery

Turn the swatches over

Six materials, no veneers, no stains. Tap or hover a swatch for the spec we quote from. Every board is picked at the mill in Ghent, twenty minutes from the bench.

Dovetails, hand-cut

Every drawer corner. Half-blind at the front so the joinery shows only when you open it — a private detail.

Mortise & tenon

Drawbored with riven oak pegs. The joint tightens as the wood moves; nothing to work loose, nothing to re-glue.

Sliding dovetail shelves

Shelves lock into the carcase sides and still breathe with the seasons. No shelf pins, no sag, no rattle.

Custom commissions

From sketch to delivery in twelve weeks

About half our year is commissions — dining tables to fit awkward rooms, built-ins, one memorable 14-foot conference table. The process is the same every time.

Week 0

Consult

An hour at the showroom or on a call. Bring room dimensions and photos. We talk budget out loud, early.

Week 2

Drawings & quote

Scaled drawings, a wood sample pack in the mail, and a fixed price. Two revision rounds included.

Weeks 4–10

The build

Your piece goes on the schedule board. We send photos at milling, joinery, and finishing — no chasing us.

Week 12

Delivery

White-glove within 150 miles of Hudson. We level it, oil it once more in place, and leave you the repair kit.
