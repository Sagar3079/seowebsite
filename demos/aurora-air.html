One fare, few rules

Every ticket includes a 20 kg bag, seat selection and free changes up to 24 hours before departure (150 kr inside 24 hours). If weather cancels your flight — it happens up here — you choose: next flight, full refund, or a night at Clarion The Edge on us when you're stranded away from home.
