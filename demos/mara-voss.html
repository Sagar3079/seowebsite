About

Hamburg, Germany

Mara Voss (b. 1986, Rostock) has spent fourteen years photographing the thin, slow light of the North — the hour when a landscape stops performing and simply sits with you. She works on medium-format film, returns to the same places for years at a time, and prints everything herself in a shared darkroom in Hamburg's Speicherstadt.

Her plates are made in editions of eight, printed on cotton rag at 60 × 75 cm. Nothing is composited; the colour is the colour that was there, held a little longer.

She accepts a small number of editorial and place-based commissions each year, and travels between September and March, when the light is lowest.
