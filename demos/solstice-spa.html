The Circuit

Five rooms, one loop

The circuit works by contrast — heat opens, cold closes, rest resets. Three loops is a session. Listen to your body over your stopwatch.

Cedar Sauna

85°C

Finnish dry heat over river stones. Ten to fifteen minutes, lower bench first.

Herbal Sauna

60°C

Gentler heat infused with lavender and pine. Where beginners learn to sweat.

Steam Room

46°C

Full-saturation eucalyptus steam. Close your eyes; the room does the rest.

Mineral Pool

38°C

Magnesium-rich water at body-and-a-bit temperature. The social heart of the house.

Cold Plunge

7°C

Chest-deep, held at seven degrees year-round. Exhale before you step in.
