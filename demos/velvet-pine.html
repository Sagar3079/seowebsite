The process

Twelve months, in order.

12 months out

The long lunch

We meet — usually over something braised — and talk about the day you're imagining, the budget you actually have, and whether we're the right fit. No contract until we all sleep on it.

10 months out

Venue & the big three

We lock the venue, photographer, and caterer first; everything else bends around them. You'll see three vetted options for each, never a firehose of forty.

8 months out

The design book

A single bound document: palette, florals, paper, light, tablescape. When your florist and your stationer both work from the same page, the day looks like one hand made it.

4 months out

Tastings & fittings

The fun part. Menu tasting, cake tasting, final fittings — we schedule it all and sit beside you taking notes you'll never have to read.

1 month out

The handoff

Every vendor confirmed in writing, a minute-by-minute timeline nobody but us needs to see, and a rehearsal we run so your officiant doesn't have to.

The day

You get married

We arrive before the florist and leave after the band. If something goes sideways — something always goes sideways — you will hear about it in the toast we give at your first anniversary, not before.
