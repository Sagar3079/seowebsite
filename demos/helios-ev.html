Design story

Drawn by light.

Every Helios begins as a single stroke — the gold line our designers pull from the front splitter, over the roof, to the rear blade. The studio calls it the heliograph. The body panels exist to hold it still at 200 mph.

That discipline shows up in the wind tunnel. At 0.197 drag coefficient, the GT is the slipperiest production car sold in America — no pop-out handles, no wing, no tricks. Just a shape that wants to keep going.

“We didn't design a car and then chase the drag number. We drew the cleanest line we could and built a car underneath it.” — Mara Ellison, Chief Designer
