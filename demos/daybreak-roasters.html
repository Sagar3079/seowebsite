The brew guide

Our V60, in four moves

This is exactly how we cup-and-serve pour overs at the bar. It's forgiving — if your timing drifts fifteen seconds, the coffee will forgive you too.

  1. 00:00 – 00:45

    Bloom

    Rinse the filter, add 22 g of medium-fine grounds, and pour 50 g of water just off the boil. Swirl once and let it puff up and burp.

  2. 00:45 – 01:15

    First pour

    Pour in slow circles to 150 g, keeping the spiral about a coin's width from the paper. The bed should stay level.

  3. 01:15 – 01:45

    Second pour

    Top up to 360 g in one steady pour, then give the brewer a gentle swirl to flatten the bed.

  4. by 03:00

    Drawdown

    Aim for a flat bed and a finished cup by three minutes. Running long? Grind coarser next time. Short? Finer.
