How it works

Four hops, one round trip.

01 · Ingest

mTLS termination, key auth, and per-org rate limits at the edge nearest the caller.

02 · Classify

A distilled embedder fingerprints the prompt for cache lookup and route policy.

03 · Route

Cache hit returns in single-digit milliseconds; misses go to the best-scoring pool.

04 · Serve

Tokens stream back through the same edge, and the cache learns the answer.
