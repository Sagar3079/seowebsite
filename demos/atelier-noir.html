A note from the atelier

Fewer, better,
longer.

"We remove until the garment stops asking for attention — and starts holding it."

Atelier Noir was founded in 2019 by pattern-cutter Lien Verhaeren and tailor Amadou Cissé. Every piece begins as a toile on a living body, not a sketch on a screen. We work with two mills — one in Biella for wool, one in Kyoto for silk — and we visit both every year.

Nothing is discounted, because nothing is overproduced. When a run of sixty sells through, the pattern rests until we can improve it.
